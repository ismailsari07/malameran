# Design brief — draft

Status: **draft, not yet agreed.** Discuss and revise before handing to a design tool.

## What this is

The public-facing site of a managed global sourcing company. A visitor arrives with a
manufacturing need — a hotel fit-out, a restaurant chain rollout, a production run —
and has to believe within five seconds that this company will handle it for them.

## Who it speaks to

Business buyers: procurement managers, developers, operators, founders. Sourcing
internationally is risky and they know it. They have been burned by unreliable
suppliers, unclear pricing, quality surprises, or have heard from someone who has.

They are not looking for a catalogue. They are looking for someone to hand the
problem to.

## The one message

Tell us what you need. We find it, verify it, and manage the process.

Everything on the page serves that sentence.

## What it must not look like

- Not a marketplace or product catalogue. No product grids, no "browse suppliers",
  no search bar as the hero element.
- Not a generic SaaS landing page. No dashboard screenshot hero, no "trusted by"
  logo strip with logos that do not exist yet, no gradient blobs.
- Not a freight or logistics company. Avoid the container-ship-at-sunset cliché.

## Character

Credible, calm, competent. This is a company that handles serious money on someone
else's behalf. It should feel closer to a professional services firm than to a
startup — but modern, not corporate-stale.

Restraint over decoration. Generous whitespace. Confident typography doing most of
the work. Colour used sparingly and deliberately.

## Pages

Home, How It Works, Services, Industries, For Suppliers, About, Contact,
privacy policy, terms.

## Home page structure

1. Hero — the promise, one line, and two actions: start a sourcing request,
   become a supplier
2. The problem — finding a supplier is easy, finding the right one is not
3. How it works — five steps, scannable
4. Services — end to end, as a list, not eight paragraphs
5. Industries — breadth without pinning the business to one sector
6. The main conversion block — can't find what you're looking for? Tell us.
7. Supplier side — join the network
8. Closing call to action

## Constraints

- English only
- Must work at 375px and 1440px
- Two primary actions site-wide: submit a sourcing request, apply as a supplier.
  Nothing should compete with them.
- No logo or brand assets yet — design should survive a logo being dropped in later
- No real photography available. Design must work with type, colour and layout
  alone, or with abstract/illustrative elements that do not require a photo library.

## Open questions

- Colour direction — is there any existing brand preference from the client?
- Serif or sans for headings? Serif reads more established, sans reads more modern.
- Is an abstract visual language wanted at all, or type and layout only?
