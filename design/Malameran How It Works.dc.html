How it works

Five steps. Two of them are yours.

You brief us and you approve. Everything between those two moments is our job, and you are told where it stands in writing.

Typical timeline
Shortlist3–5 days
Quotation1–2 weeks
Sample2–4 weeks
Production4–10 weeks
Freight and clearance2–6 weeks
Ranges depend on product, tooling and shipping mode. You get dated estimates with your quote.
01
You

Tell us what you need

A drawing, a spec sheet, a competitor's product or a photo. If you have a target price and a delivery window, tell us those too.

Specification, drawing or sample photo
Target price and quantity
Certifications the market requires
02
Us

We source and verify

We build a shortlist against your spec, then visit and audit before we recommend anyone. You see a handful of factories that can actually do the job — not a directory export.

Capability and capacity check
On-site visit and audit report
Business licence and export document review
03
You

You approve quote and sample

One quotation with landed cost, lead time and payment terms — and a physical sample in your hands. Nothing goes into production until both have your sign-off.

Landed cost, not factory-gate price
Dated lead time and payment schedule
Sample shipped to you for approval
04
Us

We manage production and quality

Progress checks on the floor, written updates at agreed intervals, and a pre-shipment inspection against the approved sample before anything is packed.

Scheduled progress reports
Pre-shipment inspection with photos
Defects resolved before shipment leaves
05
Us

We handle shipping and documents

Freight booked, export paperwork prepared, customs cleared and delivery arranged to your dock. One invoice, one accountable party.

Sea, air or consolidated freight
Export documents and HS classification
Customs clearance and final delivery
What you can expect

Commitments we make in writing.

A named specialist
