The problem

Finding a factory is easy. Finding the right one is the hard part.

Directories and trade shows will hand you a hundred names in an afternoon. None of them tell you which factory can actually hold your tolerance, your finish, or your delivery date.

The risk sits in the months after the order — the sample that passes and the shipment that doesn't, the paperwork that holds a container at the border. Someone has to be accountable for all of it. That is the job we take.

How it works

Five steps. You are only involved in two of them.

01
You

Tell us what you need

A specification, a drawing, or a photo and a target price.

02
Us

We source and verify

Shortlisted factories, visited and audited before you see them.

03
You

You approve quote and sample

Landed cost, lead time and a physical sample in your hands.

04
Us

We manage production and quality

Progress checks on the floor and inspection before shipment.

05
Us

We handle shipping and documents

Freight, export paperwork, customs clearance and delivery.

Your two touchpoints

Steps 01 and 03 need you. The other three run without you, and you are told where they stand.
