Services

Take the whole chain, or the part you cannot cover.

Most buyers hand us everything from specification to delivery. If you already have a factory, we can step in at audit, inspection or freight instead.

Full service

Eight jobs we do so you do not have to.

01

Supplier research

We read your spec first, then search. You get a shortlist of factories with the right process, tolerance range and volume band — with the reason each one is on the list.

Deliverable: shortlist with capability notes
02

Factory verification

Someone walks the floor. We check equipment, staffing, quality process, licences and export history — and we tell you what we did not like.

Deliverable: audit report with photos
03

Quotation and negotiation

We price the whole thing: unit cost, tooling, packaging, freight, duty and clearance. Then we negotiate terms in the supplier's language and time zone.

Deliverable: landed-cost quotation
04

Samples

Ordered, chased, checked against your spec and shipped to you. If the first sample misses, we run the correction loop with the factory, not with you.

Deliverable: approved physical sample
05

Production monitoring

Written progress updates at agreed milestones, with photos from the floor. Delays are reported when we learn them, not when the ship is missed.

Deliverable: milestone progress reports
06

Quality inspection

Pre-shipment inspection against the approved sample and an agreed AQL. Nothing is packed and shipped while a defect is open.

Deliverable: inspection report and sign-off
07

Freight and logistics

Sea, air or consolidated, booked at rates we hold with our carriers. Tracking and revised arrival dates come to you without being asked for.

Deliverable: booking, tracking, arrival plan
08

Export documents and customs

Commercial invoice, packing list, certificates of origin, HS classification and clearance. Paperwork errors are what hold containers, so we own them.

Deliverable: cleared shipment, delivered
