For suppliers

If you manufacture well, we want you on the list.

We place orders on behalf of buyers in Canada and the United States. Audited factories in our network get briefed on real projects with approved budgets — not price-fishing enquiries.

What we look for

Four things we check before we brief you.

01

Real capability

Your own production, your own equipment, and honest limits on what you can and cannot hold.

02

Documents in order

Business licence, export history and the certifications your product category requires.

03

Quality process

Written inspection steps, records we can read, and openness to third-party inspection.

04

Communication

Replies within a working day and bad news delivered early. This matters as much as price.
